# Voice

Derived from published posts, not aspiration. Regenerate after every five posts:
`postiee search <term>` prints the full body of anything recorded locally.

## What the writing actually does

**Hooks are short, declarative, and specific.**
- "I got my token count wrong for weeks. The fix was two lines."
- "181 Claude Code skills on my machine, and no way to see which ones were actually on."
- "Five bugs. None of them threw an error."
- "I got tired of opening files by hand."

Two sentences at most. One concrete number or one tension. No throat-clearing.

**Paragraphs are one or two lines.** White space is part of the format. A wall
of text is off-voice.

**Numbers are measured, and the measurement is named.**
"1,292 transcripts / 869 MB — cold scan 9.2s" and "the same 1,292 files again —
0.13s". Specific beats impressive. If a number has no source, it does not go in
the post.

**The limitation is stated out loud.**
"Not a UI. The part that survives when someone asks 'and then what'."
"MIT licensed. No account. Nothing leaves your machine."
Honesty about scope is the credibility move; hiding it is the tell of a
marketer.

**Structure is bullets and arrows.**
`•` for features, `→` for measurements and paths, numbered lists only for
lessons. Em dashes for asides, used sparingly (one or two per post).

**Endings ask a real question.**
"Did you dedup, or did you sum? Genuinely asking."
"What's the first thing you'd want to see — spend, or what's actually enabled?"
Never "Thoughts?" or "Agree?".

**Hashtags: 3–5 at the very end.** Lowercase and uppercase both appear; stay
consistent within a post. `#claudecode #devtools #opensource #ai`.

**Links go at the end**, on their own line, after the value is delivered.

## What is not in the voice

- Emoji as punctuation. An occasional 🤝 in a personal post is the ceiling.
- "Excited to announce", "thrilled to share", "humbled".
- Rhetorical questions in the hook ("Ever wondered why…?").
- Third-person corporate voice. This is first person, singular.
- Fake vulnerability arcs ("I failed. Here's what I learned.").
- Tagging people who did not contribute.

## Register

A builder talking to other builders, slightly dry, allergic to hype. Confident
about what was measured, explicit about what was not. The reader should feel
they could clone the repo and check.

## Sentence rhythm

Mostly short. One long sentence is allowed per paragraph, usually the one
carrying the technical detail. If three sentences in a row are the same length,
break one.
