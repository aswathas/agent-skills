# Pillars

Five pillars. The ratio is per ten posts, not per week. Deviating for a launch
or a hackathon is fine; drifting for a month is not.

| # | Pillar | Share | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Build log** | 30% | What was shipped, what broke, what it cost | proof of work, highest comment rate |
| 2 | **Teardown** | 20% | One mechanism explained from the inside | saves and reposts, earns follows |
| 3 | **Open source** | 20% | Contributions to other people's projects | borrows their audience, builds credibility |
| 4 | **AI news take** | 20% | The day's news + a practitioner angle | reach, discovery, timeliness |
| 5 | **Personal / event** | 10% | Hackathons, prizes, people, lessons | the account reads as a person |

## 1. Build log

A project from `assets/projects.md`. The shape: hook → what it does → one
number → one limitation → link.

Never a feature list. The reader wants to know what was hard and what you chose
not to do.

## 2. Teardown

One mechanism, explained. "Why the same API message can appear twice in a
transcript" is a teardown. "10 tips for Claude Code" is not.

The test: could a reader reproduce the finding from the post? If not, it is a
summary.

## 3. Open source

Contributions to repositories you do not own. Name the maintainers, link the
PRs, state what the fix changed. This pillar converts other people's credibility
into yours — but only if the work is real and the credit is accurate.

Rule: if someone else wrote the merged commit, say so.

## 4. AI news take

The news is the first line. The rest is the practitioner angle: what it changes
for someone running agents in production, what it does not change, what to
check before believing the benchmark.

Never a summary thread. Never "10 AI tools you need". If there is no first-hand
angle, skip the story and post from another pillar.

## 5. Personal / event

Hackathons, prizes, teammates, travel, failure. This is the pillar that makes
the other four credible. It should be 10% and it should be specific — the
detail, not the trophy.

## Choosing a pillar

Ask: does the material have a number or a mechanism?
- number + shipped → build log
- mechanism → teardown
- someone else's repo → open source
- headline + experience → news take
- people → personal

If it has neither a number nor a mechanism, the post is not ready.
