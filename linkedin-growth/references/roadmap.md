# Roadmap: 1K → 10K → 100K → 1M

Follower count is a lagging indicator. Each stage has a leading metric that
gates it, and one thing that stops working when you try to carry it forward.

## Stage 1 — 0 to 1,000

**Goal:** become legible. A stranger lands on the profile and knows what you
build in five seconds.

| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Posts per week | 3 |
| Comments on others' posts | 5 per weekday |
| Profile headline | says what you build, not a job title |
| Featured section | 2 repos, 1 write-up |

**Gate to stage 2:** 3 consecutive posts above 2,000 impressions, and 50+
profile views per week.

**Stops working here:** posting into the void. At this stage the comments you
leave on other people's posts bring more followers than your own do.

## Stage 2 — 1K to 10K

**Goal:** find the two formats that work for this account and repeat them.

| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Posts per week | 4 |
| Formats tested | 6 (build log, teardown, carousel, news, list, personal) |
| Document carousels | 1 per week |
| Reply window | 30 minutes after posting |

**Gate to stage 3:** one format clears 10,000 impressions twice, and the
follower count grows more than 8% in a month.

**Stops working here:** variety for its own sake. Once a format proves out,
repeating it is not laziness, it is positioning.

## Stage 3 — 10K to 100K

**Goal:** become the person people cite on one narrow subject.

| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Posts per week | 5 |
| Named series | 1 (e.g. "Harness notes", weekly) |
| Collaborations | 1 per month (co-post, interview, joint teardown) |
| Repurposing | every strong post becomes a doc, a README section, or a thread |

**Gate to stage 4:** inbound DMs asking for help or work, and 3 posts above
100,000 impressions.

**Stops working here:** reacting to news. At this size the audience expects a
point of view, and a take that could have been written by anyone stops
travelling.

## Stage 4 — 100K to 1M

**Goal:** own a category, not a feed.

| Lever | Target |
|---|---|
| Cadence | daily, or a fixed weekly slot never missed |
| Newsletter | 1, one per week or two |
| Media | talks, podcasts, recorded demos |
| Team | an editor or researcher for the pipeline |

**Gate:** not a number. The gate is that the account can survive a month without
posting and still grow, because the work is being cited by others.

**Stops working here:** doing it alone. Volume at this scale requires a process
and usually a second person.

## The metrics that matter at every stage

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Impressions per post | distribution; if this is flat, the hook is the problem |
| Comments per 1,000 impressions | the strongest signal of interest |
| Profile views | intent; people checking who you are |
| Followers per post | conversion from reach to audience |
| Saves / reposts | whether the post has reference value |

**Do not optimise for likes.** They are the cheapest signal and the easiest to
fake, which makes them the least useful for growth.

## Cadence honesty

The number that matters is posts per week that survive the gate. Four posts that
pass beat seven that are written to fill a slot. If a week has no material, post
a teardown from the archive rather than inventing a lesson.
